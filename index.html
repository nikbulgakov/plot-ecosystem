how it works⌄

What you see is a simulated little world.

The weather changes over time, influencing the actions of the inhabitants and the vibe of the musical layer.

Each agent represents a creature or an object in the world, with its own rules and rhythms.

What you hear and can follow in the log is the result of their interactions and actions translated in real time into a generated sound.

The sky is real: time, weather, temperature and wind come from the place you are in (or one you pick), so no two plots are alike.

The plot keeps living while you are away. Creatures have names, grow old, are born and die, and new ones wander in. Come back after a few days and read what happened.

Drag to orbit · wheel to zoom · click a creature to follow it · click the place to move the plot.
