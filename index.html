How it works

A small simulated wild place: a verge, a few stones, a dead oak and the creatures that live there.

The sky is real. Time, weather and wind come from where you are, or from a place you pick, so no two verges are alike.

Every creature has a name and its own rules. What you hear is what they do, turned into sound as it happens.

The verge keeps living while you are away. Creatures grow old, are born and die, and new ones wander in.
